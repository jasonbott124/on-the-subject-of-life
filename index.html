    What is On the Subject of Life*?

    A long-form podcast featuring guests from every field and background, talking about their work, their research, their motivations, and the advice they'd give.

    The goal is a library of thought-provoking conversations: knowledge that helps people succeed and feel fulfilled, and a spotlight on inspiring research and ideas that don't get the attention they deserve.

    The show launched in 2023 as Heights of Humanity; you'll still find it under that name on some platforms. It has no affiliation with the University of Texas or UTIG. It's simply an effort to make quality conversations with real experts free for anyone.

    About Jason

    Hi, I'm Jason, a particularly well-traveled geophysics undergrad at UT Austin and a research associate at the University of Texas Institute for Geophysics, deeply curious about geology, glaciology, wildlife conservation, history, language… all of it, frankly, just Life. This podcast is my attempt to share the discussions I have about Life and its many facets, and to keep an archive of the people and topics I find fascinating, however niche.

    Where can I find the podcast?

    On YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and most other podcast apps. See Listen & watch for the full list.

    What format can I expect?

    Long-form conversations, usually one to two and a half hours, released in both audio and video.

    Where can I find past episodes?

    Every episode is in the Episodes section above. Filter by topic or search for a guest.

    How do I recommend a guest?

    Send a note to heightsofhumanity@gmail.com with who they are and why they'd make a great conversation.

    Where can I send feedback?

    Constructive feedback and criticism are always welcome at heightsofhumanity@gmail.com. Nonconstructive feedback, threats, or hateful speech won't be reviewed.
